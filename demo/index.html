lightbox

An image viewer where pictures grow out of their thumbnails, crop and all. Swipe between them, drag down to close, pinch or double-tap to zoom. Plain TypeScript, no dependencies. Tap any picture below.

npm install github:drkxbt/lightbox

A gallery

Square tiles, a wide one and a tall one. Each picture grows out of exactly the part its tile shows.

Big pictures, small thumbnails

These thumbnails are 640 pixels wide; the full screenshots are 2880. The thumbnail shows at once and the full image takes over when it has loaded. Zoom in to read the numbers.

On its own

An image with an empty data-lightbox opens by itself, with no counter or arrows. Round, square or cropped, it grows from its own shape.
